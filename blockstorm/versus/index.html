Tetris-style multiplayer you can start in ten seconds

BlockStorm Versus is a free online 1v1 falling-block battle played in the browser — synthwave neon, CRT glow and a deep teal board, in the lineage of Tetris, Tetrinet and Multris. No download, no account, no install: one player creates a room, sends the link or the four-character code, and the match begins as soon as the other one arrives.

How a versus match works

Both boards draw from the same bag of pieces in the same order, so nobody gets a luckier sequence — the result is the player's, not the shuffle's. Clearing lines sends garbage rows to the other board: a double sends one, a triple two, a tetris four, and a T-spin more again. Back-to-back difficult clears and combos add to it, and a perfect clear sends ten.

Incoming garbage does not land straight away. It waits in a queue along the edge of your board, and clearing lines of your own while it waits cancels it — so a well-timed tetris turns their attack into nothing instead of into a hole you have to dig out of. Whatever is left rises from the floor on your next placement, with a single gap to clear through.

Best of three

A single game ends quickly once lines start crossing, so a match is best of three: first to two wins, and a double top-out is replayed rather than counted. Between games you can send an emoji, see the set score, and start the next one without leaving the room.

Controls

Arrow keys or WASD to move and rotate, space to hard drop, and the whole thing also works with a game controller — D-pad or stick to move, A to rotate, up to hard drop — and with touch on a phone. The guideline rules are here: SRS wall kicks, a seven-bag randomiser, lock delay, T-spin detection, combos and back-to-back.
