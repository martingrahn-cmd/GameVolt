Draw your way up in INK

INK is a free arcade game where you draw the flipper yourself. Blue ballpoint strokes, orange bumpers and highlighted green bricks turn a sheet of squared notebook paper into a vertical pinball course.

How to play

Drag with your finger or mouse to draw a stroke below the ball. The ball can bounce off each stroke once. Drawing spends ink and slows time; collect blue ink blots and break green blocks to refill. Avoid red spikes and keep the ball from falling off the bottom of the page. Chain hits without drawing again to build your combo.

Free climb or today's challenge

Climb endlessly with checkpoints every 100 metres, or take on the daily 500-metre course with a changing rule. Records and 31 trophies save in this browser without an account. Use the Pause button whenever you need a break.
