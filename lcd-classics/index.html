    LCD Classics — Free Online Retro LCD Handheld Games

    LCD Classics is a collection of three pocket LCD handhelds in the spirit of the early-80s Game & Watch, set in a neon synthwave showroom: a striped sunset sun, a rolling pink-and-cyan grid floor, a chrome logo and square-wave beeps. Each handheld is a game of its own, drawn the way real LCD games worked — every figure sits in a fixed spot on the glass, the unlit segments show faintly as ghosts, and everything moves in ticks rather than smooth animation.

    Dock Drop

    A wide-screen handheld in tugboat red. Three delivery drones let go of parcels, and your little robot on a raft moves between three spots to catch them before they splash into the harbour. A pirate drone sneaks in and drops bombs you must not catch. Energy cells charge a magnet that catches everything for a few ticks, and ten catches in a row raise the score multiplier up to x4.

    Crush Yard

    A yellow two-screen clamshell. On the lower screen the whole scrapyard slides one step at a time in the same direction, and anything in front of a scrap cube gets shoved along. Get pinned against the edge and you are crushed. On the upper screen a crane operator points where the scrap goes next, and a warning lamp blinks before the direction changes. Five energy cells give you a BLAST that clears the cubes around you.

    Girder Jack

    A blue two-screen clamshell. Climb a crane tower past rolling oil drums, some of which tip down the ladders, and jump two drums at once for a combo. At the top, time your jump for a swinging crane hook: four hooks pull four bolts and drop the Foreman, and every drop raises the level and the pace.

    How to Play

    Browse the handhelds with the arrow keys, a swipe or a gamepad, and press Enter or A to pick one up. Inside a game, Game A is the normal pace and Game B is faster. Keyboard: arrow keys move, Space is the action button, 1 and 2 start Game A and B, T shows the high score and Esc returns to the shelf. Gamepad: D-pad or stick to move, A for action, Start for Game A, X for Game B and Select to go back. On a phone, tap the buttons on the handheld or tap the screen.

    Trophies and Leaderboards

    The collection has 31 trophies — 15 bronze, 10 silver, 5 gold and one platinum for collecting the rest — and a leaderboard for each handheld in Game A and Game B. Every trophy can be earned solo. Sign in to GameVolt to keep your trophies and post your scores.
