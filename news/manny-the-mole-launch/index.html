New game: Manny the Mole — dig deep, crack the vault

Manny the Mole is live — a Mr. Driller-style arcade digger about going down on purpose. Manny has spent a lifetime tunneling under banks, and these twelve shafts are his last job before retirement. Matched blocks crumble in clumps, the ceiling follows you down, and the air meter is the only clock that matters.

Dig like you mean it

Every shaft is a hand-carved descent: seams that collapse in chains, heavy blocks that punch your air tank on the way down, gold worth doubling back for, and side pockets where a tank of air waits for whoever dares the detour. Clear a level and the next one digs deeper — with gold, silver and bronze medals on every shaft for the fast hands.

Crack the circuit

At the bottom of every shaft sits a vault, sealed with a circuit lock. Uncover conductors, swap them into a route, and get the current from IN to OUT — while the current itself creeps up behind your fingers. The full 24-lock campaign is playable straight from Puzzle mode, every lock a fixed board with a medal time. And if a lock beats you, it wears down: the current runs slower every retry, so persistence always gets you through.

One lock a day, for everyone

The Daily Lock is the same board for every player in the world, fresh at midnight. Solve it daily to keep your streak alive — miss a day and the count starts over, but your best streak is remembered forever. Sign in and your streak goes on the global leaderboard, next to the deepest run scores.
