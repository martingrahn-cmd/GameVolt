Chrome Circuit is live — isometric arcade racing, rockets optional

Six chunky low-poly cars, a track seen from above like a toy box come to life, and a pack that never quite lets you go. Chrome Circuit, our isometric arcade racer, is out now and playable free in your browser.

Chrome Circuit is built in the spirit of Rock 'n Roll Racing and Super Off Road: a fixed isometric camera, short sharp races and contact that matters. Five rivals line up beside you on every grid, and the whole race is on screen at once — who is coming up the inside, who just picked up a rocket, and how close the next corner is.

Twelve circuits across four worlds

City Lights runs from the friendly Downtown Loop past the armco of Harbour Sprint to the long straights of Neon Speedway. Country Roads winds through blossom on Sakura Hills, over the crests of Greenhill Valley and into the forest on Pinecrest Rally. Alpine Winter climbs to black ice on Glacier Pass and Summit Run, and Red Rock Canyon ends the tour with Mesa Eight — a figure eight that crosses over itself on a stone arch bridge — the gravel of Dust Bowl Rally and the jumps of Canyon Run.

Drift, boost, fire

  • Drift boosts — hold the handbrake into a corner and the car commits to the slide. Sparks at the back wheels turn blue, then orange; let go and the kick is bigger for orange.
  • Rocket starts — hit the gas just as the lights go green. Too early and the wheels just spin.
  • Items — turbos, rockets that nudge toward whoever is ahead, and oil drums to drop behind you for whoever is following.

A career of cups, and a ghost to beat

The career is a cup in each world: its three circuits in turn against the same five rivals, with points for every place, on Rookie, Pro, Ace or Legend. Finish a cup in the top three and the next world opens, along with new cars — ten in the garage when you have them all. Win every world's cup on Pro or harder and you have beaten the game.

Time trial puts you alone on the track against the ghost of your own best lap, with bronze, silver and gold medal times on every circuit. Set a lap you are proud of and send it as a link: your friend races your ghost, and can send theirs straight back.

Made for a controller

Every menu works from a gamepad, and the game shows the buttons of the pad in your hands — Xbox, PlayStation or Switch — in its pause screen, How to play and a new Controls page. Pads with rumble feel the lights, the contact and the turbo. It is also in GameVolt Big Picture, our controller-first library for the living room. Keyboard works too, and on a phone the car drives itself while your thumbs steer and drift.

31 trophies, free, no download

Thirty-one trophies run from Green Light for your first finish, through Drift King and Gold Rush, to Chrome Circuit Master. Sign in free and they follow you to your GameVolt profile. Chrome Circuit runs directly in the browser on desktop, tablet and phone, installs as an app if you want it to, and has no ads and no signup. The soundtrack is Somewhat Good: Karts by flowerhead.
