LCD Classics is live — three pocket LCD handhelds in an 80s showroom

A striped sunset sun, a neon grid rolling toward you and three little handhelds standing on it like toys in a shop window. LCD Classics is our tribute to the pocket LCD games of the early 80s, and it is out now, free in your browser.

LCD Classics is one game with three handhelds inside it, each a complete game of its own. They are drawn the way real LCD games worked: every figure sits in a fixed spot on the glass, the unlit segments show faintly as grey ghosts, and everything moves in ticks to a square-wave beep. Each one has a Game A for the normal pace and a faster Game B, three misses end a run, and when nobody is playing the screen shows a clock and runs a demo.

Dock Drop — catch it before it splashes

A wide-screen handheld in tugboat red. Three delivery drones hang over a harbour and let go of parcels, and a little robot on a raft moves between three spots to catch them. From 25 points a pirate drone joins in and drops bombs you must not catch. Energy cells charge a magnet that catches everything for a few ticks, gold parcels are worth five, and ten catches in a row lift the multiplier — up to x4. Reach 200 and 500 points and your misses are wiped.

Crush Yard — don't get squeezed

A yellow two-screen clamshell. On the lower screen the whole scrapyard slides one step at a time in the same direction, and anything standing in front of a scrap cube gets shoved along with it. Get pinned against the edge and you are crushed. On the upper screen a crane operator points where the scrap goes next, and his warning lamp starts blinking a few ticks before the direction changes. There is always an open path through the scrap, but it winds. Five energy cells give you a BLAST that clears the cubes around you, and gears are worth ten points.

Girder Jack — climb to the hook

A blue two-screen clamshell. Climb a crane tower past oil drums rolling down the girders, and jump two of them at once for a combo. Watch the ladders: a drum that tilts at the top is about to come down it. At the top, time your jump for the swinging crane hook. Miss it and you drop a floor, dazed, but it does not cost a life. Catch it and a bolt comes loose — four bolts and the Foreman falls off his perch, the level goes up and so does the pace.

A showroom to browse

The handhelds stand in a 3D cover flow on a neon grid floor under a striped sunset, to a looping synthwave track by kontinuum. The two-screen clamshells stay closed until they reach the middle, then flip their lids open; pick one and the camera dives onto it until the handheld fills the screen, ready to play. The demos stay silent on the shelf, so only the game you are playing beeps.

Trophies, leaderboards and controls

  • 31 trophies — 15 bronze, 10 silver, 5 gold and 1 platinum, from Batteries In through Foreman Down and Unsquishable to LCD Classics Master. Every one can be earned solo.
  • Six leaderboards — one for each handheld on Game A and Game B.
  • Keyboard, gamepad or touch — arrow keys and Space, a controller everywhere including the menus, or the buttons on the handheld itself on a phone.

Free, instant, no download

LCD Classics runs directly in the browser on desktop, tablet and phone, with no app to install, no ads and no signup. Sign in free and your trophies and scores follow you between devices.
