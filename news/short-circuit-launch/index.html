Short Circuit is live — close the route before the current does

A junction panel of covered tiles, a piece of conductor hidden under each one, and a current that is already moving. Short Circuit, our circuit-board puzzle game, is out now and playable free in your browser.

Short Circuit asks one thing of you: get an unbroken route from IN to OUT before the current gets there first. Every tile on the board hides a piece of conductor — straights and corners. Tap to uncover them, work out where each piece belongs, then tap two conductors to swap their positions. The covered grid comes from Minesweeper, the connection logic from pipe-laying puzzles, and the tension comes from the fact that the current never waits. It advances while you think, so every tile you uncover costs time you might need for the swap.

Twenty-four locks, and they grow

The campaign is a 24-lock junction box. The first lock runs on a deliberately slow current and walks you through every move; after that the box runs at full speed. Boards grow as you go — the deepest panels reach 6×6 — and some conductors arrive welded in place, answering a swap with a dead clank and forcing you to route around them. Each lock keeps gold, silver and bronze medal times, so a lock you have already opened is still worth coming back to.

Losing lowers the wall

Fail a lock and it does not get harder — it gets easier. Every retry wears the mechanism down and the current runs 20% slower on the next attempt, up to roughly double the time at the cap. Persistence always gets you through the door; the medal times are what bring you back to do it cleanly.

A daily lock, and live duels

  • Daily Lock — one board a day, identical for every player, so times are directly comparable. Keep it going and your streak builds; there are trophies at 3, 7 and 30 days, and the streak has its own global leaderboard.
  • Live Duel — a real-time 1v1 on an identical circuit. First to close it wins. Host a room or send an invite link.
  • 31 trophies — 15 bronze, 10 silver, 5 gold and 1 platinum, from First Spark through All Panels Open to Master Electrician.

Where it came from

If the circuit lock feels familiar, it should. It started as the safecracking puzzle at the bottom of every shaft in Manny the Mole, and it kept being the part people replayed. Short Circuit is that puzzle lifted out and built into a game of its own — same engine, given room for a full campaign, a daily board and an opponent.

Free, instant, no download

Play with touch, mouse, or the arrow keys and Enter — R restarts a lock and Esc backs out. Short Circuit runs directly in the browser on desktop, tablet and phone, with no app to install, no ads and no signup. Sign in free and your progress, trophies and streak follow you across devices.
