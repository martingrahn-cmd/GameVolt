Slipstream Vector — Free Online Anti-Gravity Racing Game

Slipstream Vector is a fast anti-gravity arcade racer with sunset megacities, neon coastlines and a sharp retro-futurist feel. Pilot a hovering machine through luminous tunnels, storm-lit industrial zones and high-speed coastal circuits where clean lines matter as much as raw acceleration.

Twelve circuits across four worlds

The championship spans twelve purpose-built tracks across four distinct worlds. Straights open into banked turns, split routes, loops, corkscrews and jumps. Each circuit asks you to learn its rhythm, choose the faster line and keep the ship stable while the speed class rises.

Arcade racing with rivals and weapons

Slipstream Vector combines immediate arcade handling with a full racing field. Draft behind opponents to build a slipstream, use airbrakes to tighten your line and collect weapons that can change the order in seconds. Rival pilots have their own teams, personalities and strengths, giving the championship the feel of a futuristic racing league.

Championship, single race and time trial

Enter the championship to climb through its cups and unlock faster classes, choose a single race for a quick battle, or take on time trial and chase a cleaner lap against your own best performance. Records, trophies and progression are built into the same in-game interface.

Play with gamepad, keyboard or touch

The game has full controller navigation and racing support, including analogue steering and rumble on compatible gamepads. Keyboard controls are rebindable, and touch driving controls let phones and tablets steer, thrust, brake, drift and fire. You can also launch it from GameVolt Big Picture for a controller-first living-room experience.

Free in your browser

Slipstream Vector runs directly in a modern web browser with no download, purchase or signup required. Play as a guest, or sign in to GameVolt to connect trophies and supported progress to your profile.
