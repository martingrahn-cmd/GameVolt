How to Play Sudoku

Fill the 9×9 grid so every row, every column and every 3×3 box contains the digits 1 through 9 exactly once. Tap a cell, then tap a number — the puzzle always has a solution you can reach by pure logic. You're allowed three mistakes, so think before you commit.

  • Pencil notes — toggle Notes to jot candidate digits in a cell. Correctly placed numbers automatically clear related notes.
  • Undo — step back through every move, including notes.
  • Auto-save — close the tab mid-game and resume exactly where you left off.
  • Keyboard play — arrow keys to move, 1–9 to fill, N for notes, Ctrl+Z to undo.

Sudoku FAQ

Is this Sudoku really free?

Yes. 100% free, no ads, no signup needed. Sign in only if you want cloud-synced trophies and a place on the global leaderboard.

Is the daily challenge the same for everyone?

Yes — every player gets the same daily puzzle, generated from a date-seeded random number generator. Complete it to build your streak and compare times on the daily leaderboard.

What do the difficulty levels mean?

Easy starts with 38 given numbers, Medium with 30 and Hard with just 24. Fewer givens mean more empty cells to deduce, longer solving chains and a bigger challenge.

What happens if I make three mistakes?

The game ends. Every wrong number you place counts as a mistake, so think before you commit — or use pencil notes to keep track of candidates. Win with zero mistakes to earn the Flawless trophies.

How do pencil notes work?

Toggle Notes and tap numbers to jot small candidate marks in a cell. When you place a correct number, that candidate is automatically cleared from the surrounding row, column and box.

Can I pause or resume a game?

Yes. Your game auto-saves after every move, so you can close the tab and pick up exactly where you left off. Press Escape during a game for the pause menu.
