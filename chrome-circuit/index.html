      How to play

      Career

      The career is a cup in each world: its three circuits in turn against the same five rivals, points for every place. Finish the cup in the top three and the next world opens, and so do new cars. Win every world's cup on Pro or harder and you have beaten the game. Single race and time trial open every circuit.

      Drift boost

      Hold the handbrake into a corner with the wheel turned and the car commits to the slide: steering now only tightens or widens the arc. Sparks at the back wheels turn blue, then orange — let go and you get a kick of speed, bigger for orange. Run onto the snow or the grass and the charge drains away.

      Rocket start

      Hit the gas just as the lights go green for a turbo off the line. Too early and the wheels just spin.

      On a phone

      The car drives itself. Slide a thumb anywhere on the left to steer — the further it slides, the harder you turn. Hold anywhere on the right to drift; let go for the kick. ★, bottom left above your item, fires it — a drift holds its line while your left thumb taps it. Touch the right side as the lights go green for a rocket start.

      Items

      • TURBO A short, glorious overdose of speed.
      • ROCKET Fires forward and nudges toward whoever is ahead.
      • OIL DRUM Drops behind you. Whoever hits it, spins.

      Grass and dirt will slow you down and steal your grip. Barriers hurt more.

      Chrome Circuit — Free Online Isometric Racing Game

      Chrome Circuit is a bright isometric arcade racer with chunky low-poly cars, in the spirit of Rock 'n Roll Racing and Super Off Road. Six cars to a race tear through sunlit city streets, blossom-lined country roads, snowy alpine passes and red rock canyons, seen from above like a toy box come to life.

      Twelve circuits across four worlds

      City Lights, Country Roads, Alpine Winter and Red Rock Canyon each hold three circuits, from the friendly Downtown Loop to Neon Speedway's long straights, the ice on Glacier Pass, the gravel of Dust Bowl Rally and Mesa Eight, which crosses over itself on a stone arch bridge. Some climb and fall, some have crests to fly off, and most have no barriers to save you.

      Drift, boost and fire

      Hold the handbrake into a corner and the car commits to a slide; sparks at the back wheels turn blue, then orange, and letting go kicks you out of the turn with a burst of speed. Hit the gas just as the lights go green for a rocket start. Item boxes hand out turbos, homing rockets and oil drums to drop behind you.

      A career of cups, single races and time trials

      The career is a cup in each world: its three circuits against the same five rivals, with points for every place, on Rookie, Pro, Ace or Legend. Finish a cup on the podium to open the next world and new cars. Single race opens every circuit at once, and time trial puts you against the ghost of your own best lap, with bronze, silver and gold medals to win — and a link to send your ghost to a friend to beat.

      Play with gamepad, keyboard or touch

      Chrome Circuit has full controller support, from the menus to the race, and shows the buttons of the pad in your hands — Xbox, PlayStation or Switch — with rumble on pads that have it. Keyboard works too, and on a phone the car drives itself while you steer and drift with your thumbs. Launch it from GameVolt Big Picture for a controller-first living-room session.

      Free in your browser

      No download, purchase or signup: Chrome Circuit runs in any modern browser and can be installed as an app. Play as a guest, or sign in to GameVolt to keep your 31 trophies on your profile. The soundtrack is Somewhat Good: Karts by flowerhead.

      Chrome Circuit FAQ

      Is Chrome Circuit free to play?

      Yes. Chrome Circuit runs in your browser with no download, purchase or signup. Signing in to GameVolt, which is also free, only saves your 31 trophies to your profile.

      Can I play Chrome Circuit with a controller?

      Yes, it has full controller support from the menus to the finish line. On an Xbox-style pad the right trigger or A accelerates, the left trigger brakes, the stick or D-pad steers, the right bumper drifts, X or Y fires your item and Start pauses. The on-screen prompts match your pad, Xbox, PlayStation or Switch, and the game is part of GameVolt Big Picture for playing on a TV.

      How do you drift and boost in Chrome Circuit?

      Hold drift (the right bumper, Shift on a keyboard) into a corner and the car commits to a slide. Sparks at the back wheels turn blue, then orange; let go on the way out and the car kicks out of the turn with a burst of speed, bigger the longer you held it. Hit the gas just as the lights go green for a rocket start.

      What are the keyboard controls?

      W and S or the up and down arrows accelerate and brake, A and D or the left and right arrows steer, Shift drifts, Space uses your item, Esc or P pauses and F toggles full screen.

      Can I play Chrome Circuit on a phone?

      Yes. On a touchscreen the car accelerates by itself: slide your left thumb to steer, hold anywhere on the right to drift and let go for the boost, and tap the star to use your item. It can also be installed as an app.

      How many tracks and cars does Chrome Circuit have?

      Twelve circuits across four worlds, City Lights, Country Roads, Alpine Winter and Red Rock Canyon, and ten cars. Eight are open from the start; two karts are won by finishing the City Lights and Country Roads cups on the podium.

      Can I race against my friends?

      Races are single-player, against five AI rivals on Rookie, Pro, Ace or Legend. In time trial you can send a friend a link to your ghost, so they race your best lap on their own screen.
