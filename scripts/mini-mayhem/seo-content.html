Mini Mayhem — Free Online 3D Mini Golf Game

Mini Mayhem is a bright, toy-like 3D mini golf game with chunky low-poly courses and a mischievous streak: brass clockwork towns, floating cloud-forest islands, a carnival on the moon and a haunted harbour under an orange moon. Twenty-four holes, each with a trick of its own, and most with a daring shortcut beside a safer way round.

Four worlds, 24 holes

Clockwork City is a miniature town in a clockmaker's workshop: a brass gate that lifts, tube mail up to a terrace, a bridge lowered by a golden bell and a giant clock hand to cross on the Stroke of Twelve. In the Cloud Forest you jump to a landing pier and bowl down pins, dodge a swinging acorn, loop through a giant root and fly through the eye in the Mother Tree's trunk. The Moon Carnival has rocket pads, a zero-gravity circus tent, a black hole to slingshot around, a comet cannon, a Ferris wheel that carries your ball half a turn up to the green, and a broken moon rocket to fire from. Together they make the 18-hole tournament.

Haunted Harbour, for Halloween

The fourth world is played on its own: six holes on rickety piers over black water. Wait for the green light under a crypt's portcullis, take a run up a seesaw plank, bounce off jack-o'-lantern bumpers past a spinning scarecrow, keep your line down a slippery slime chute and ride a ghost cannon. The finale flies you aboard a ghost ship whose rotten planks drop away in rhythm.

Three taps to a good shot

Aim by dragging over the course, set the power, then stop the needle in the green zone for a straight shot. Near the hole on a flat green the meter counts in metres for the putt. A birdie or better earns three stars, par two and a bogey one. Mouse, touch and keyboard all work: A and D fine-tune the aim, Shift adds precision, Space or Enter takes each step and Esc cancels.

Play with up to four friends

Open a room, share the code, and up to four players join on their own phones or computers. The host picks the holes and the mode: everyone plays at once with the others' balls shown as ghosts, or turn by turn in a random order that rotates every hole. Emoji reactions say what words can't.

Trophies and leaderboards

Thirty-one trophies wait to be won, from your first hole to a hole in one. Each world has a leaderboard for the world played in a row: the fewest strokes wins, and on the same strokes the faster time does, counted only while you can play. Play as a guest, or sign in to GameVolt to keep your trophies on your profile and your rounds on the boards. The game speaks English, Swedish and Spanish.

Mini Mayhem FAQ

Is Mini Mayhem free to play?

Yes. Mini Mayhem runs in your browser with no download, purchase or signup. Signing in to GameVolt, which is also free, saves your 31 trophies to your profile and puts your rounds on the leaderboards.

How do you swing in Mini Mayhem?

A swing takes three taps. Aim by dragging over the course, set the power on the meter, then stop the needle in the green zone for a straight shot. Near the hole on a flat green the meter counts in metres for a putt. On a keyboard, A and D fine-tune the aim, Shift adds precision, Space or Enter takes each step and Esc cancels.

Can I play Mini Mayhem with friends?

Yes. Up to four players join a room with a code, each on their own device. The host picks the holes and the mode: everyone plays at once with the others' balls shown as ghosts, or turn by turn in a random order that rotates every hole. Players can send emoji reactions.

How many holes does Mini Mayhem have?

24 holes in four worlds of six: Clockwork City, Cloud Forest and Moon Carnival make up the 18-hole tournament, and Haunted Harbour is a Halloween world played on its own.

How do the leaderboards work?

Each world has its own board for the world played in a row. The fewest total strokes wins, and on the same strokes the faster time wins. The clock only runs while you can play.

Can I play Mini Mayhem on a phone?

Yes. It plays in portrait or landscape with touch: drag to aim and tap the big button for each step of the swing. Mouse and keyboard work on a computer. The game is in English, Swedish and Spanish.
